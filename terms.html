Terms of use

Last updated November 11, 2026. Luxora is a prototype, so these pages describe how the prototype behaves.

1. What Luxora is

Luxora is a prototype AI shopping assistant. The products and brands you see are real, and the prices were read from retailers’ own pages on different days (November 3 or 4, 2026); each price shows its own checked date and may change. Prices, stock and deals can change at any time. Product pictures are illustrative images, not official photos, and may not match the real product’s finish. Luxora’s own checkout is currently a Stripe test-mode experience and is not yet a live retail order system.

2. Amazon Associates disclosure

As an Amazon Associate, Luxora earns from qualifying purchases. Where Amazon or other retailer links are shown, the retailer’s own price, availability, privacy policy and terms apply and may differ from the information Luxora displays.

3. Stripe test checkout

Luxora’s checkout currently uses Stripe test mode. Test payment details may be processed by Stripe, but no real money is charged, no real retail order is created and nothing is shipped. A Stripe test payment reference is not an order number. Luxora should not be used for real purchases until the checkout is explicitly moved to live mode and order fulfillment is enabled.

4. No subscriptions or billing

Luxora has no paid plans, subscriptions or billing of any kind.

5. Your account

Creating an account asks for your full name, email, phone, a password, country, street address, an optional apt/unit, city, state/region and ZIP/postal code. Your account is stored in your own browser, and your password is stored there only as a one-way salted hash, never in plain text. The Privacy policy lists the few details that are sent to Luxora’s sign-up form provider. This is still a prototype account, so do not rely on it to protect anything sensitive.

6. Assistant replies

The assistant gives suggestions from a small catalogue of 13 products. Replies may be incomplete or wrong, so please do not treat them as professional advice.

7. Acceptable use

Please do not try to disrupt the site or submit other people’s personal details to the deals sign-up form.

8. Changes

The prototype can change or disappear at any time, and these terms may be updated as it does.
