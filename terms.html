Terms of use

Last updated November 3, 2026. Luxora is a prototype, so these pages describe how the prototype behaves.

1. What Luxora is

Luxora is a prototype AI shopping assistant. The products and brands you see are real, and the prices were read from the retailers’ own pages on November 3, 2026 (the date is shown next to each price). Prices, stock and deals can change at any time, so always confirm at the retailer. Luxora does not sell anything, and the “Buy at” buttons open the retailer’s site in a new tab, where its own terms apply. “Original price” comparisons use the price the retailer shows or the manufacturer’s listed price, and some of those discounts have been running for a long time. Luxora has no verified customer ratings, so it shows none. Product pictures are illustrative images, not official photos, and may not match the real product’s finish.

2a. Amazon Associates disclosure

As an Amazon Associate, Luxora earns from qualifying purchases. “Buy now” buttons open Amazon.com and contain Luxora’s Amazon Associates tag. Amazon’s own price and availability apply when you buy, and may differ from the prices Luxora shows.

2. No real orders or payments

Luxora’s own cart and checkout are a demonstration. Luxora does not take payment, place an order or ship anything. Any order reference you see is not a real order.

3. No subscriptions or billing

Luxora has no paid plans, subscriptions or billing of any kind.

4. Your profile

The sign-in is a prototype: it only saves a name and email in your own browser. It is not a secure account and there is no password. Do not rely on it to protect anything.

5. Assistant replies

The assistant gives suggestions from a small catalogue of 13 products. Replies may be incomplete or wrong, so please do not treat them as professional advice.

6. Acceptable use

Please do not try to disrupt the site or submit other people’s personal details to the deals sign-up form.

7. Changes

The prototype can change or disappear at any time, and these terms may be updated as it does.
