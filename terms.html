Terms of use

Last updated November 3, 2026. Luxora is a prototype, so these pages describe how the prototype behaves.

1. What Luxora is

Luxora is a prototype AI shopping assistant. The products, brands, prices, deals and reviews you see are sample data and are not real offers.

2. No real orders or payments

Checkout is a demonstration. Luxora does not take payment, place an order or ship anything. Any order reference you see is not a real order.

3. No subscriptions or billing

Luxora has no paid plans, subscriptions or billing of any kind.

4. Your profile

The sign-in is a prototype: it only saves a name and email in your own browser. It is not a secure account and there is no password. Do not rely on it to protect anything.

5. Assistant replies

The assistant gives suggestions from a small sample catalogue. Replies may be incomplete or wrong, so please do not treat them as professional advice.

6. Acceptable use

Please do not try to disrupt the site or submit other people’s personal details to the deals sign-up form.

7. Changes

The prototype can change or disappear at any time, and these terms may be updated as it does.

8. Contact

Questions? Use the contact details under Help & support in Settings. The contact address there is a placeholder for now.
