Privacy policy

Last updated November 3, 2026. Luxora is a prototype, so these pages describe how the prototype behaves.

What stays on your device

Your profile (name and email), settings, chat history, saved items and cart are stored in your browser’s local storage. They are not sent to Luxora servers (only your chat messages are, as described below), and there is no Luxora account database. You can download or erase all of it under Settings → Privacy & data.

What can leave your device

  • Deals sign-up form. If you submit it, the name and email you type are sent to our sign-up form provider, Forminit, and used only to send Luxora deals and updates. It is opt-in, and the box must be ticked before anything is sent.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs such as IP address and browser type.
  • Chat messages. When you chat, your message and the ids of the products last shown (so follow-ups like “which is cheaper?” work) are sent to the Luxora assistant service, a free Cloudflare Workers function. It answers from the product list and does not store your messages or know who you are. If the service can’t be reached, the assistant answers inside your browser instead and nothing is sent. Cloudflare may keep standard request logs.
  • Retailer links. If you press a “Buy at” button, the retailer’s site opens in a new tab and that retailer’s own privacy policy applies from then on. The links are marked as sponsored as a precaution.
  • Bug reports and support. These open an email in your own mail app. Nothing is sent unless you press send there.

What we don’t do

No advertising, no tracking cookies, no selling of data, and no payment details are ever collected.

Your choices

Turn off deal emails, clear your chat, saved items or cart, download your data, or delete your profile at any time in Settings. Clearing your browser’s site data also removes everything stored locally.
