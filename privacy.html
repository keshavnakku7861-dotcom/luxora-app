Privacy policy

Last updated November 11, 2026. Luxora is a prototype, so these pages describe how the prototype behaves.

What stays on your device

Your account (name, email, phone, address, and your password as a one-way hash), settings, chat history, saved items and cart are stored in your browser’s local storage. Your password never leaves your device, and there is no Luxora account database; the only parts that are sent anywhere are listed below. You can erase all of it by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings, and you can log out under Settings.

What can leave your device

  • New accounts. When you create an account, your name, email, phone number, city, state/region and country, plus the time you signed up, are sent to Luxora’s sign-up form provider, Forminit, so Luxora knows who has joined. Your password, street address and ZIP/postal code are not sent.
  • Deals sign-up form. If you submit it, the name and email you type are sent to our sign-up form provider, Forminit, and used only to send Luxora deals and updates. It is opt-in, and the box must be ticked before anything is sent.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard web server logs such as IP address and browser type.
  • Chat messages. When you chat, your message and the ids of the products last shown (so follow-ups like “which is cheaper?” work) are sent to the Luxora assistant service, a free Cloudflare Workers function. It answers from the product list and does not store your messages or know who you are. If the service can’t be reached, the assistant answers inside your browser instead and nothing is sent. Cloudflare may keep standard request logs.
  • Amazon links. “Buy now” buttons open Amazon.com and carry Luxora’s Amazon Associates tag so Amazon can tell the visit came from Luxora. As an Amazon Associate, Luxora earns from qualifying purchases. Amazon’s own privacy notice applies once you open Amazon.
  • Retailer links. If you open a retailer link, that retailer’s own privacy policy applies from then on. Sponsored or affiliate links are identified where applicable.
  • Stripe test checkout. Luxora currently uses Stripe in test mode. Payment details entered in Stripe’s secure Payment Element are sent directly to Stripe. Luxora does not receive or store your full card number. The current prototype creates only test payments; no real money is charged and no real order is created. Contact and shipping details entered in Luxora’s checkout remain in the page for the current checkout session and are not stored in a Luxora order database.
  • Bug reports and support. These open an email in your own mail app. Nothing is sent unless you press send there.

What we don’t do

No selling of personal data. Luxora does not receive or store full card numbers; Stripe handles payment details inside its secure payment interface. The current checkout is test mode only and does not create real charges or orders.

Your choices

You can log out in Settings at any time. Clearing your browser’s site data removes everything stored locally.
