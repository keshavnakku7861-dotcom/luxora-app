LUXORA HELP

How can we help?

Browse a topic below, or search. For a quick question, you can also ask in the Assistant.

Orders & checkout

  • Luxora checkout runs in Stripe test mode, so no real order is created and there is nothing to track. For a purchase made on Amazon through a Luxora link, use your Amazon account.

  • When you buy on Amazon through a Luxora link, the order is placed with Amazon and handled by the seller. Amazon’s own price, availability and terms apply.

  • Buy now on a product page opens checkout in a new tab with only that item. Checkout in the cart opens it with everything in your cart. There are four steps: Address, Payment, Review and Confirmed. Prices are checked again by Luxora’s server before any payment. Because checkout is in test mode, the confirmation shows a Stripe test reference, not an order number.

  • Your cart is saved in this browser. You can add up to 9 of each item, and rings need a ring size. Buy now does not change your cart.

Shipping & delivery

  • Luxora checkout is in test mode, so nothing is shipped from it yet. Purchases made on Amazon through a Luxora link are fulfilled by Amazon or the seller.

  • Delivery for Amazon-link orders is handled by Amazon or the seller, under their own delivery terms.

  • For an Amazon-link order, check its status in your Amazon account. In Luxora checkout you choose Standard ($9.99, free when your subtotal after any promo code is $150 or more) or Express ($19.99); in test mode nothing is shipped.

Returns & refunds

  • Returns are handled by the retailer or seller you bought from, under their own return policy.

  • For anything bought on Amazon through a Luxora link, Amazon’s or the seller’s return policy applies. Start the return from your Amazon account.

  • Luxora checkout payments are Stripe test payments, so no real money is charged. For retailer-link purchases, refunds follow that retailer’s own policy.

Promo codes

  • Type the code and press Apply. RAM2016 gives 15% off full-price items. Upper or lower case both work.

  • Use the promo bar at the top of the app, or the promo field in checkout.

  • Items already on a deal keep their deal price; a code does not discount them again.

  • Clear the code in the promo bar or in checkout. One code can be active at a time, and an invalid code is removed before payment.

Payments

  • Card, through Stripe’s Payment Element in test mode. To try it, use a Stripe test card such as 4242 4242 4242 4242. No real money is charged.

  • When you buy on Amazon through a Luxora link, you pay Amazon directly. As an Amazon Associate, Luxora earns from qualifying purchases.

  • Card details go directly to Stripe. Luxora never receives or stores your full card number.

Account & sign-in

  • Use Continue with Google, or create an account with your email and a password.

  • Use the account button to sign in, view your account or log out.

  • Edit your name, phone and address in Settings. Email and password accounts are stored in your own browser.

FAQ

Is Luxora a store or a shopping assistant?

Luxora is an AI shopping assistant. You can browse products in the app, and some purchases are placed through Amazon links. When you buy through a retailer link, that retailer’s own price, availability and terms apply.

How do Buy Now links work?

Buy now on a Luxora product page opens Luxora’s checkout in a new tab; it runs in Stripe test mode, so no real money is charged. Buy-on-Amazon links go to Amazon. As an Amazon Associate, Luxora earns from qualifying purchases. Orders placed through Amazon links are handled by the seller.

How do promo codes work?

Enter a code in the promo bar at the top of the app. One code can be active at a time. A code may not apply to items that are already on a deal.

How do I contact Luxora?

Use Ask Luxora in the Assistant — you can start a chat from the Contact Luxora card on this page, or open the Assistant in the sidebar.
