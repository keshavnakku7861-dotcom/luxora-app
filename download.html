Install Luxora

Add Luxora to your phone so it opens from your Home Screen like an app.

Open Luxora

iPhone / iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the Chrome menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Confirm the installation.

Luxora is currently installed from the web. It is not yet listed in the Apple App Store or Google Play Store.
